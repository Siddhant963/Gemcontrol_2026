# Off-Page SEO & Social Distribution Plan

This is a strategy document, not something the codebase implements --
off-page SEO happens outside the website itself, through genuine business
activity and content distribution. No backlinks, profiles, or mentions
described here have been created; this is a plan for the RatnSetu/ADRS
Techno team to execute.

## Principles

- Every link or mention should come from a real, relevant relationship or
  genuinely useful content -- not a purchased placement.
- Relevance to jewellery retail / Indian SMB software beats raw link volume.
- Never use: paid spam backlinks, PBNs (private blog networks), automated
  backlink tools, comment spam, irrelevant directories, link farms, mass
  guest-post packages, or fake profiles/reviews. These violate Google's
  spam policies and put the whole domain at risk, not just the linking page.

## 1. Owned-property mentions (do first -- zero cost, fully within your control)

- **ADRS Techno's own website** (adrstechno.com): add RatnSetu as a listed
  product/case study with a link to ratnsetu.com. This is a legitimate,
  accurate relationship (RatnSetu is genuinely developed by ADRS Techno).
- **LinkedIn Company Page** for RatnSetu (or a product page under ADRS
  Techno's existing company page if one exists) -- link to ratnsetu.com in
  the profile, post product updates and blog links.
- **RatnSetu social presence** (Instagram/Facebook/YouTube as capacity
  allows) -- same principle, link back to the site in bio/about sections.

## 2. Content-driven mentions

- **Product launch post/announcement**: a short write-up of what RatnSetu
  is and why it was built, shared via ADRS Techno's and RatnSetu's own
  channels, and submitted to relevant Indian startup/product directories
  where genuinely applicable (e.g. general SaaS/startup listing sites --
  vet each one for relevance and real traffic before submitting, don't
  mass-submit to low-quality directories).
- **Jewellery industry publications/blogs**: identify 3-5 genuinely
  relevant Indian jewellery trade publications or business blogs and pitch
  a guest article or product mention -- framed around a real problem
  (inventory, Udhaar, GST billing), not as a thinly-veiled ad.
- **YouTube educational content**: short demo/walkthrough videos ("How to
  track Udhaar in your jewellery shop," "Setting up gold rate management"),
  which double as genuinely useful content and a natural place to link back
  to relevant blog posts and the pricing page.

## 3. Relationships and case studies

- **Genuine customer case studies**: once real customers exist and agree,
  a short case study (with their permission, real name/shop) is far more
  valuable than any generic testimonial -- replace the sample testimonials
  on `/testimonials` with these as they become available (see
  `src/data/testimonials.js`).
- **Partner mentions**: if RatnSetu integrates with or is resold alongside
  any other jewellery-industry tool or service, a mutual mention/link is
  legitimate and relevant.
- **Founder/company mentions**: interviews, panel appearances, or press
  mentions of ADRS Techno / the RatnSetu team in business/tech media, where
  they occur naturally.

## 4. Relevant business directories

Only directories that are (a) actually browsed by Indian jewellery
retailers or SMB software buyers, and (b) not obviously a link-selling
scheme. Examples worth evaluating on their own merits: general Indian
business/SaaS directories, industry association listings if RatnSetu or
ADRS Techno is a member of one. Skip anything that looks like it exists
purely to sell backlinks.

## 5. Social content calendar (topics, not a ranking tactic)

Social posts don't directly move Google rankings, but they distribute blog
content to people who might link to or search for RatnSetu -- that's the
actual SEO value.

**Platforms**: LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube.

**Topics to rotate through**:
- Jewellery inventory tips
- Jewellery shop management tips
- Gold rate management
- Business digitization ("registers to ERP")
- Customer management
- Udhaar management
- RatnSetu feature walkthroughs / short demos
- Product updates

Each post should link back to the relevant page or blog article on
ratnsetu.com rather than just describing the feature in the post itself.

**Suggested hashtags** (social platforms only -- hashtags are not a Google
ranking factor and should never be added to the website itself):
`#RatnSetu #JewelleryERP #JewellerySoftware #JewelleryBusiness
#JewelleryRetail #JewelleryShop #BusinessManagement #RetailTechnology`

## What NOT to do

- Do not buy backlinks or link packages of any kind.
- Do not submit to directories in bulk or ones unrelated to jewellery/SMB
  software.
- Do not fabricate reviews, case studies, or customer logos.
- Do not create fake social profiles to link back to the site.
